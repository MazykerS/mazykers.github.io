JARVIS

A private personal assistant for a single user. Not a public service.

JARVIS is a personal, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner, the author of the YouTube channel @mazyker. It helps plan gaming videos and reviews the channel's own statistics.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the owner's own YouTube channel statistics through the official YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Read-only access only: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly.
  • It never uploads, edits, deletes or publishes anything, and never manages comments.

No other users can sign in. The app is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

JARVIS — личный ассистент одного владельца канала @mazyker. Доступ к данным YouTube только на чтение собственной статистики канала; ничего не публикует и не изменяет. Политика конфиденциальности.
